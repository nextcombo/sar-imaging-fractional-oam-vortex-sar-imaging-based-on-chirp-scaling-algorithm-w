# [SAR Imaging] Fractional OAM Vortex SAR Imaging Based on Chirp Scaling Algorithm [with MATLAB Code]

Introduction: The vortex electromagnetic waves carrying orbital angular momentum have received significant attention from researchers [1]. Due to the orthogonality of OAM in different modes, vortex electromagnetic waves in different modes are independent of each other. Therefore, they have significant application potential in high-bandwidth data transmission and are promising for research in wireless communication, electromagnetic imaging, SAR imaging, and other fields.
In [6], Mr. Guo from the National University of Defense Technology in China first applied vortex electromagnetic waves to radar imaging and pointed out their great potential in target azimuth direction imaging, providing a new approach for the development of new radar systems. In [7], Mr. Liu K. derived the echo signal models in both multiple input multiple output (MIMO) and multiple input single output (MISO) modes, and obtained the two-dimensional focused results of targets through fast Fourier transform (FFT) and BP imaging algorithm. In [8], Mr. Liu H. proposed an electromagnetic vortex enhanced imaging method based on fractional OAM beams to combat noise interference, and introduced a method for generating fractional OAM beams with non-integer OAM mode phase distribution using uniform circular arrays, analyzing its characteristics. In [9], based on the side-looking stripe SAR imaging model, Mr. Wang J. used the RD algorithm to obtain the target imaging results. Theoretical and simulation results indicate that compared to traditional SAR technology, the side-looking stripe SAR technology can more clearly characterize the targets in the beam. In [10], Mr. Zheng H. reviewed the latest progress in theoretical models, transmission, generation, measurement, and applications of fractional vortex beams, and considered future possible directions and challenges.
In recent years, research on integer-order OAM vortex SAR imaging has achieved many results. From the current research situation, fractional-order OAM vortex SAR imaging is of significant importance. Based on this, this paper first derives the imaging echo formula for side-looking strip SAR, then selects the Chirp Scaling algorithm to encode the echoes. Finally, simulation experiments were conducted on the detection targets. The experimental results show that in the multi-target and noise environment, fractional-order OAM vortex SAR imaging has better robustness than integer-order imaging, providing a feasible reference solution for electromagnetic vortex SAR imaging.

Conclusion: This paper establishes the imaging geometric model and echo signal model of fractional-order OAM vortex SAR. Based on the Chirp Scaling imaging algorithm, the effectiveness of the fractional-order OAM vortex SAR algorithm was verified through point target simulation experiments. In the multi-target imaging scenario, the imaging cross section under different OAM modes shows that fractional-order is more robust than integer-order. This is because the first Bessel function in the integer-order vortex SAR imaging echo contains a certain degree of influence on the imaging performance of point targets. Both theoretical and simulation results indicate that fractional-order vortex SAR imaging has strong robustness in noisy environments. As the SNR increases, the imaging performance deteriorates, which is the result of severe interference of noise signals with the imaging signals. Additionally, fractional OAM can fully utilize the number of OAM modes. Therefore, compared with traditional vortex SAR imaging methods, the fractional-order OAM vortex SAR imaging method has stronger robustness.
